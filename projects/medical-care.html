MediCare — Hospital App Design Concept

Role: UI/UX Designer Type: Personal / Concept Project Year: 2025
MediCare — Design Concept

Mockups coming soon — this case study will be updated with Figma screens and prototype link once available.

Overview

MediCare is a UI/UX concept for a hospital and medical care app, designed to make booking appointments and accessing medical information simpler for patients.

The Problem

Patients often struggle to book appointments, find the right doctor or department, and keep track of past visits and prescriptions across disconnected systems.

Design Process

As the UI/UX Designer, I researched common patient frustrations with hospital apps, then designed wireframes and a full hi-fi UI in Figma focused on getting patients to the right doctor and appointment slot with minimal steps.

Key Screens

  • Doctor and department directory
  • Appointment booking flow
  • Medical records and visit history
  • Appointment reminders and notifications

Tools

Figma

Outcome

The concept simplified the patient journey from finding a doctor to booking an appointment into a streamlined few-step flow, demonstrated through an interactive Figma prototype.
