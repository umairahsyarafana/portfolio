Washhouse — Laundry Pickup & Delivery Management System

Role: Full Stack Developer Type: Academic Project Year: 2025

Overview

Washhouse is a web and mobile-based platform that streamlines laundry service operations. It allows customers to schedule pickups, track the status of their orders, and manage payments — replacing what was previously a manual, phone-call-driven process with a structured digital workflow.

The Problem

Local laundry services typically rely on manual booking and tracking, leading to missed pickups, scheduling conflicts, and no clear visibility into order status for either the customer or the business. The goal was to design a system that removes that friction on both sides.

My Role & Approach

As the Full Stack Developer on this project, I worked across both the design and engineering of the system — from mapping out user flows and screens in Figma, to building the mobile app in Flutter and the backend in PHP and MySQL, with Firebase handling real-time order updates.

Key Features

  • Customer-facing pickup and delivery scheduling
  • Real-time order status tracking
  • Integrated payment management
  • Dedicated driver app for managing pickup and delivery tasks
  • Admin dashboard for managing orders and operations
