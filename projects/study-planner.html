Study Planner — Mobile App Design Concept

Role: UI/UX Designer Type: Personal / Concept Project Year: 2025

Overview

Study Planner is a UI/UX concept for a mobile app that helps students plan their study schedules, track assignments, and stay on top of deadlines in one organized space.

The Problem

Students often juggle deadlines across multiple subjects with no single place to plan study time, leading to last-minute cramming and missed assignments.

Design Process

As the UI/UX Designer, I researched common student planning habits and pain points, then sketched low-fidelity wireframes before designing a full hi-fi UI and interactive prototype in Figma.

Key Screens

Onboarding & Login

Study Dashboard

Assignment & Deadline Tracker

Reminder & Notification Settings

Overall App Flow

Tools

Figma

Outcome

The concept resulted in a clean, low-friction planning flow that prioritizes the next deadline at a glance, validated through a clickable Figma prototype.
