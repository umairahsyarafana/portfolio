WajibPakai — Modern E-Commerce Platform for Tudung Products

Role: Web Developer Type: Academic Project Year: 2025

Overview

WajibPakai is a complete online shopping platform built for tudung products, covering the full shopping journey — product browsing, cart management, secure checkout, and administrative tools for managing the storefront.

The Problem

Small fashion retailers often lack an easy way to sell online without relying on third-party marketplaces. The goal was to build a dedicated, user-friendly storefront that gives full control over the product catalog, orders, and customer experience.

My Role & Approach

As the Web Developer on this project, I built the platform end-to-end — from the product catalog and cart logic to the checkout flow and admin-side product management, using ASP.NET with a SQL Server database.

Key Features

  • Product catalog with browsing and search
  • Shopping cart and order management
  • Secure checkout process
  • Admin panel for managing products and orders
